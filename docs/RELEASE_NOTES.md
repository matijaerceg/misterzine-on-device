**Fixed**

- On a CRT over direct video with the menu set to 50 Hz (menu_pal=1), MisterZine filled only the middle 60% of the screen. It now fills the width, the way the menu does. Thanks to fandenivoldsk for reporting it.
- A card whose files came from Downloader alone, such as through MiSTer Companion, showed a MisterZine menu entry that only reloaded the menu over a blank picture. The entry now appears once Setup has run from Scripts, and an existing card keeps its entry after updating.

Full details in the [changelog](https://github.com/matijaerceg/misterzine-on-device/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#v114--2026-09-24).
