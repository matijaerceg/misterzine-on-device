**Games stay playable while the catalogue updates**

**Fixed**

- With **Found on card** selected, your games stay on screen and ready to launch while MisterZine checks a newly downloaded catalogue against the card. Thanks to berp for reporting the wait.
- A card scan that fails or stops partway now retries by itself, even offline. **Rescan card** still retries right away.
