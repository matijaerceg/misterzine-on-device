Compare list layouts at a glance

Highlight List layout in Options to see List, Split and Picture together. Clear diagrams show where the game list, artwork and metadata will sit, adapting to your horizontal or tate screen. Use Left/Right to choose; the border follows your selection. Move to another option and the previews disappear.
